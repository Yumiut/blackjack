How to play

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Number cards count at face value, face cards count as 10, and an ace counts as 1 or 11.

Hit to draw a card. Stand to let the dealer play. On your first turn, double down to match your bet, take one final card, and stand.

Split two cards of the same rank by placing an equal bet. Each hand gets one more card. Play the left hand first, then the right. You can split once per round. A split-hand 21 pays even money.

The dealer draws to 17 and stands on soft 17. A win pays even money. A two-card blackjack pays 3:2. A tie returns your bet, including when both hands have blackjack.
